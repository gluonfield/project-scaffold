# Relay

> Status updates your team will actually read.

Relay demonstrates a calmer way for teams to share what shipped, what is blocked,
and what is next in one short daily digest. No meetings, no digging through
channels.

## Why Relay

- **Async by default:** Post an update in under a minute, whenever it fits your day.
- **One daily digest:** A single morning summary, grouped by project. Fewer pings,
  more clarity.
- **Stay in sync:** A shared view of progress, wherever your team's day takes them.

## Product overview

The product concept brings progress and blockers together so team leads can see
where someone needs a hand. The landing page previews a fictional team's updates.
The [interactive demo](/demo) lets visitors add an update to that sample digest.

This site is a demonstration. It does not provide a connected team workspace,
scheduled digest delivery, or account registration.
