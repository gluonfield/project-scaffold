# Try Relay

> One small update. Everyone caught up.

Try the [sample workspace](/demo) without an account or payment details. Updates
stay in the current page session and reset when you reload. They are not saved to
a server or shared with other people.

## Add an update

1. Choose a project: Website or Mobile app.
2. Choose a status: Shipped, Blocked, or Up next.
3. Write a short update of up to 240 characters. Empty or whitespace-only updates
   are not accepted.
4. Select **Add update**. The new entry appears at the top of the digest as **You**.

The digest shows the number of updates, distinct projects, and blockers. The
**Reset sample** button restores the original three entries. JavaScript is needed
to add updates; the sample digest remains readable without JavaScript.

## Sample digest

The workspace is called Studio. Its initial entries are fictional examples:

- **Maya — Website — Shipped:** The new homepage is live. Ready for the team to
  take a look.
- **Alex — Mobile app — Blocked:** Waiting on the final design review before the
  next release.
- **Sam — Website — Up next:** Testing the new onboarding flow with the team
  tomorrow.

The initial digest contains three updates, two projects, and one blocker.

## Product guide

- **Choose your project:** Give your update a home so the right people can find it.
- **Share what matters:** Say what shipped, flag a blocker, or share what's up next.
- **Read the digest:** See progress and where someone needs a hand, all in one place.

## Pricing and availability

The sample workspace is free to explore. It requires no account or payment
details. No paid plans or subscriptions are offered on this site.

The demo does not connect to external services or send email or messages to your
team. It does not retain submitted updates after a page reload.
